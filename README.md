# 🌟 Supply Chain Intelligence Suite

> **Unified portfolio showcase linking three enterprise supply chain engineering projects built by Anshul Silhare.**

### 1. [Supply Chain AI Control Tower](https://github.com/AnshulSilhare/supplier-escalation-automation)
Autonomous procurement, supplier scorecard grading, quality defect root-cause analysis, and multi-tier escalation powered by 11 n8n pipelines, Google Sheets, and Gemini AI.

### 2. DemandSense AI Forecasting Engine
Enterprise SKU-level demand forecasting system with time-series decomposition, festival impact multipliers, and dynamic safety stock optimization.

### 3. DataCo Predictive Delivery Engine
Real-time delivery delay risk classifier built with XGBoost, SHAP feature importance explainability, and carrier benchmarking.
